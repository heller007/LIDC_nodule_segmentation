## Inter-reader agreement

| quantity | mean | sd | p5 | median | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| reader vs reader (pairwise) | 0.5274 | 0.2108 | 0.259 | 0.5 | 0.897 |
| reader vs others' consensus | 0.5924 | 0.3169 | 0.0 | 0.75 | 0.917 |

**Read the second row, not the first.** Pairwise reader agreement measures how much the annotators disagree with each other. It is not the ceiling for a model, because the model is trained against the vote->=2 *fusion* of all readers, and predicting a consensus is easier than predicting any individual -- fusing averages out each reader's idiosyncratic noise.

The second row is the right comparison: each reader scored against the majority consensus of the others, which is the same kind of target the model produces.

So a model scoring much above **0.592** per-image Dice on this target is doing something a radiologist cannot do against their colleagues' consensus -- which points at leakage, at an easier metric (global rather than per-image Dice), or at fitting the fusion rule rather than the anatomy.
